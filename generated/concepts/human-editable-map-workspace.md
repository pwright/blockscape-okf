# Human-Editable Map Workspace

Blockscape should not be framed as AI automatically understanding a system. A stronger claim is: **AI proposes; humans calibrate; the map persists.**

The human editing layer is central because teams know context that AI does not. They can decide:

- an item is more visible or valuable than the AI inferred
- an item is less mature than it appears
- a dependency is wrong or missing
- a risk is understated
- an unknown should remain visible
- an item belongs in a different category

The spreadsheet-like interface matters because it makes map maintenance accessible. A team should be able to edit rows and fields without learning a specialist modeling language.

That gives teams agency over the model instead of asking them to accept AI output passively.
