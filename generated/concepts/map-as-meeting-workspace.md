# Map as Meeting Workspace

Blockscape can be framed as the shared object of attention for remote and hybrid teams.

The map is not just a post-meeting artifact. It can serve three roles:

- before the meeting: agenda and pre-read context
- during the meeting: whiteboard and editable shared model
- after the meeting: durable recap and context for follow-up

This is not the same as claiming Blockscape is a generic whiteboard. A generic whiteboard is free-form and often disposable. A Blockscape map is structured, editable, durable, and machine-readable.

The meeting goal is to reduce ceremony and increase the chance of shared understanding. Instead of talking around scattered docs, tickets, logs, and summaries, the team edits the model directly.
