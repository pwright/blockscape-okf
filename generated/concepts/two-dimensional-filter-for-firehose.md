# 2D Filter for the Firehose

Blockscape can act as a two-dimensional filter for technical information overload.

Teams are flooded with logs, documentation, tickets, chat, meeting notes, dashboards, and AI output. A map compresses that firehose into a surface where people can inspect relationships and priority.

Useful map surfaces can include:

- value versus maturity
- risk versus confidence
- visibility versus uncertainty
- dependency depth versus fragility
- urgency versus ownership

The point is not to make every axis permanent. The point is to give teams a working surface that reveals what matters, what is connected, what is unclear, and what should be inspected next.
