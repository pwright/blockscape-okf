# Anticipated Questions and Answers

## What is Blockscape?

Blockscape is a shared understanding workspace for complex systems. AI can draft structured maps from messy technical information, but teams edit and calibrate those maps into durable shared context.

## Is Blockscape just another AI summarization tool?

No. Summaries produce disposable prose. Blockscape produces structured, editable models that can be inspected, corrected, versioned, tested, and reused.

## Why maps?

Complex systems are relational. Maps expose components, dependencies, value, maturity, risk, uncertainty, and gaps in a form people can scan and challenge faster than linear prose alone.

## Why is human editing central?

AI can draft structure, but teams own the meaning. Humans know when a component is more valuable, less mature, riskier, more uncertain, or differently connected than an AI inferred.

## Why a spreadsheet-like UI?

The spreadsheet metaphor keeps map editing low-friction. Teams can edit rows and fields without learning a specialist modeling language.

## What does "2D filter for the firehose" mean?

Blockscape compresses noisy information into a map surface. Axes such as value, maturity, risk, confidence, visibility, and uncertainty help teams decide what matters and what to inspect next.

## How does Blockscape help meetings?

The map can be the agenda before the meeting, the whiteboard during the meeting, and the durable recap after the meeting.

## What are AI punchcards?

AI punchcards are compact, human-approved slices of map context that can be reused in future AI workflows.

## What should Blockscape not claim?

Blockscape should not claim that AI automatically understands systems, that maps replace human judgment, or that maps replace documentation. The credible claim is: AI drafts structure; teams own meaning.
