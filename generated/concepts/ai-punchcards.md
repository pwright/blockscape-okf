# AI Punchcards

AI punchcards are compact, reusable context objects for AI workflows.

Instead of feeding an AI tool a large pile of markdown, logs, notes, and summaries, a team can provide a small, human-approved slice of a Blockscape map.

An AI punchcard can include:

- stable ID
- item name
- dependencies
- maturity or stage
- value or visibility
- risk
- evidence
- notes
- intended use

The punchcard metaphor is useful because it suggests a durable, task-focused input. It is small enough to pass around, structured enough for machines, and reviewed enough for teams to trust more than raw generated prose.
