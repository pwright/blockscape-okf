# Shared Understanding Workspace

Blockscape is best framed as a shared understanding workspace for complex systems.

That spine is broad enough to hold the other framings without making them compete:

- AI helps draft maps from messy technical material.
- Humans edit and calibrate the meaning.
- Visual structure helps teams orient faster than prose alone.
- Maps become durable artifacts for meetings, documentation, planning, debugging, onboarding, and future AI work.

This avoids describing Blockscape as only a summarizer, a whiteboard, a diagramming tool, a knowledge graph, or a meeting agenda. Those are useful supporting frames, but the main claim is simpler: Blockscape helps teams create and maintain shared understanding.

## Product Loop

```text
AI drafts -> humans calibrate -> map persists -> people and AI reuse it
```

The map is the shared object of attention. It can be prepared before work, edited during discussion, and reused after the moment has passed.
